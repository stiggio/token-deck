# Expert review of welcome screen v5 (Nov 2, 2026) and what v6 did with it

Four independent critics (typography, composition, brand, device realism) reviewed v5 from the rendered
previews, the device photos and the brand sources. v6 (`welcome_v6.py`) applies every fix below except
where noted; each one is enforced by a check in the generator.

## Typography and legibility (verdict: ship-with-fixes)

1. **Key 3 slogan (ink x 235-291, y 37-79) and key 6 URL (x 235-275, y 158-178). Cause: ink_text() in welcome_v5.py, which draws each line with one d.text() call.** Pillow puts every glyph on a whole pixel: drawn at x+0.25, a glyph renders exactly as it does at x. Each fractional RAQM advance is rounded on its own, so the visible letter gaps vary from about 0.5 px to 1.5 px; Söhne's designed gaps are 0.6 to 1.1 px. The results: a fully empty column at x=277 that splits the word into 'Managem ent'; a soft double gap at x 246-247 ('Co nsole'); and an empty column inside both 'io.stigg.io' and '/usage'. Stem phases are also fixed per glyph. The T stem splits 41/57 % across x 237/238 and the U stem 34/63 %, so each gives off about half the light of an 'n' stem (89 %) or the T crossbar (100 %). The result is blotchy type, and the whole slogan gives off only 78 % of the light of the true Buch outline.
   Fix: Place every glyph at its exact shaped x and keep Pillow's 9.5 px vertical hinting. For glyph j of line t: x_j = 235 + font.getlength(t[:j+1]) - font.getlength(t[j]); this includes the pair kern. Draw the glyph into an 'L' mask at floor(x_j), then shift it right by phi = x_j - floor(x_j) using (1-phi)*m + phi*np.roll(m, 1, axis=1). Combine the glyph masks with max, then composite white over #131512 through a gamma-1.3 LUT: v = 255*(c*(1-b**1.3) + b**1.3)**(1/1.3), where b = the INK channel / 255. Gamma 1.3 keeps the slogan's total light at v5's level (247 vs 243 units). At 1.8 and above it starts to read bold, so don't go there. In the scratch render, in-word empty columns go from 1 to 0 in Management, io.stigg.io and /usage, Console's double gap evens out, the 7 px cap and 5 px x-height rows stay crisp, and 0 pixels land outside the tiles as burned. Keep the pen at x=235 on every line. Note that font.getbbox(t)[0] is 0 for all six lines, so the current 'align on the ink' step does nothing. That is the right behaviour: Söhne's side bearings already let the T and C overhang the U and M stems by about 0.5 px, as the type designer intended.
2. **Key 3 slogan line breaks and vertical position (4 lines, y_cap 37, LEADING 12, baselines 44/56/68/80).** 'The AI / Usage / Management / Console' splits 'AI usage', the phrase's core pair, across two lines. The rag goes short, short, long, medium (27, 27, 56, 34 px): two stub lines on top, a bulge on line 3 and an empty top-right corner. The slogan is the only tall stack in the top row, and none of its baselines matches the 'stigg' wordmark baseline in key 1 (y 63, last ink row 62).
   Fix: Break the slogan as ['The AI Usage', 'Management', 'Console']. At 9.5 px, 'The AI Usage' measures 56.23 px, exactly the width of 'Management' (56.23 px). Lines 1 and 2 therefore form one clean measure (ink x 235-291, inside tile 3's x < 292), word spaces stay two clear columns, and 'Console' closes the block. Keep LEADING 12 and set y_cap = 44 instead of the centred 43. That gives baselines at 51/63/75 and ink at y 44-74 (centre 59, against the window centre of 58.5). 'Management' then sits on y=63, the same baseline as the wordmark in key 1, so the logo and slogan share one baseline across the top row. A scratch render confirmed 0 stray pixels.
3. **Key 6 URL: the two periods on row 164 (x 242-243 and 266-267) and the '/u' join at x 237-239, y 170-174.** Each period renders as a grey smear 2 px wide: decoded values 118/184 and 127/180 on a background of about 20, i.e. 42 %/70 % coverage, which is about two-thirds of the light of one white pixel. The URL's separators are its faintest marks. Söhne's '/u' kern (-0.47 px at 9.5 px) also pushes the slash against the u stem: rows 172-173 read 228/91/162 and 176/90/161 across x 237-239, with no dark column between them, so '/u' becomes a V-shaped knot.
   Fix: Draw each '.' as a single #FFFFFF pixel on the row above the baseline, at x = round(pen + 0.51). Söhne's period is a 1.09 x 1.12 px dot with a 0.51 px left side bearing. With the current positions that gives (243,164) and (266,164). Drop the '/u' kern at this size: 'usage' starts 0.47 px further right (u pen at 235 + 3.81 = 238.81; with whole-pixel placement, u goes at x=239 instead of 238), which opens a dark column between the top of the slash and the u stem. Do not hang the slash 1 px left instead: in testing that reads as a detached '/ usage'.
4. **Key 1 logo (x 20-79, y 50-66): the LANCZOS downscale of stigg-logo-20x.png.** LANCZOS undershoot leaves a dark ring around the wordmark and the mark. As burned, 104 pixels in key 1 are darker than #131512, and 58 of them are at or below 8 in every channel (minimum 0). That is #000 right against the white letters.
   Fix: Keep LANCZOS for its crispness, but clamp the resized logo to the background before pasting: logo = ImageChops.lighter(logo, Image.new('RGB', logo.size, INK)). Downscaling with Image.BOX also works. A scratch check found 0 pixels at or below 8 in key 1 as burned. Also add a generator assert that no source pixel is below INK in all three channels.

## How v6 resolved the conflicts

- Periods: single crisp pixels at Söhne's own spacing (the merged review: no respacing), '/u' kern dropped.
- Text light: sub-pixel glyph placement plus gamma 1.5 compositing (typography suggested 1.3 to keep v5's
  light, device suggested linear light; 1.5 restores most of the outline's light without reading bold).
- JPEG halo outside the tiles: solved by quality 97 (0 window pixels outside the tiles more than 4 luma
  levels off #131512, worst 3.2) instead of moving the logo to x = 24, which would have broken the shared
  7 px margin. 15.9 KB, inside the 22.3 KB already proven on the deck.
- Token lattice: the device critic's even 6 px lattice (4 px tokens, chroma-aligned) won over pitch 5;
  column x = 20 also gives the composition critic's shared edge with the logo.
- Fan: rise 0.50 (between brand's 0.45 and composition's 0.66) with the seed chosen by the combined checks
  of both critics; 6 of seeds 1-400 pass, seed 259 chosen by eye.
- Token clip: tile rect intersected with the window shrunk by 6 px (device: keycap rim), not the tile
  itself (composition), because the rim glare is real on this unit (test 13 photo).

## Where v6 departs from the merged review below, and why

- JPEG quality 97, not 92. The review took q92 as a property of the medium because the brief described the
  current setting that way; it is a setting. At q97 no window pixel outside the tiles is more than 3.2 luma
  levels off #131512 (box and PIL decodes), with no whitelist, at 15.9 KB (22.3 KB is proven on the deck).
